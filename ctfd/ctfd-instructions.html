Join the Masterclass

Your step-by-step guide to registering and joining a team.

1

Start Registration

Find the Register button in the top navigation bar.

2

Enter Details

Fill out your User Name, Password, and the Registration Code provided to you. For the email field, just use a dummy email (e.g. your-name@example.com) — it is not verified.

3

Choose Your Path

Once registered, you must either join an existing team or create a new one to participate.

4A

Create a Team

Enter a Team Name and Password. Share these credentials with your teammate.

4B

Join a Team

Get the Team Name and Password from your teammate to join their roster.
