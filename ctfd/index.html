Join the Masterclass

Your step-by-step guide to registering and joining a team for the CTF.

1

Start Registration

Begin your journey by finding the Register button located in the top navigation bar of the Masterclass platform.

2

Enter Details

Fill out the required fields including your User Name, Email, Password, and the specific Registration Code provided to you.

3

Choose Your Path

Once registered, you must either join an existing team or create a new one to participate in the challenges.

4A

Create a Team

If you're forming a new team, enter a Team Name and Password. Share these credentials with your teammate.

4B

Join a Team

If your teammate has already created a team, gather the Team Name and Password from them to join their existing roster.

Challenge Tiers

Pick your challenges based on your comfort level. Whether it's your first time touching Kubernetes or you write manifests in your sleep, we have something for you.

Warm Up

Trainee Elf

Perfect for your first day. Get comfortable with basic kubectl commands, manifests, and reading your first vulnerability scan.

  • Orientation: YAML Debugging & Image Pinning
  • OJT: Debugging CrashLoopBackOff & Secrets
  • Graduation: Services & ConfigMaps
  • The Poisoned Present: Trivy & CVE Remediation
In the Weeds

Workshop Elf

Put your recent training to the test. Apply Dockerfile and Helm best practices, debug startup ordering, and lock down what a workload is allowed to do.

  • The Exposed Coordinates: Helm Secret Mounts
  • The Frozen Handshake: TLS Certificates
  • The Impatient Elf: initContainers & Startup Ordering
  • The Bloated Sleigh Image: Multi-Stage Docker Builds
  • The Skeleton Key: RBAC & Least Privilege
Boss Fight

Senior Security Elf

For the daily driver. Handle strict admission controllers and restore wiped stateful apps.

  • The Trojan Manifest: Kyverno Policies & Security Context
  • The Phantom Storage: StatefulSets & PVCs
