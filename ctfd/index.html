Join the Masterclass

Your step-by-step guide to registering and joining a team for the CTF.

1

Start Registration

Begin your journey by finding the Register button located in the top navigation bar of the Masterclass platform.

2

Enter Details

Fill out the required fields including your User Name, Email, Password, and the specific Registration Code provided to you.

3

Choose Your Path

Once registered, you must either join an existing team or create a new one to participate in the challenges.

4A

Create a Team

If you're forming a new team, enter a Team Name and Password. Share these credentials with your teammate.

4B

Join a Team

If your teammate has already created a team, gather the Team Name and Password from them to join their existing roster.
