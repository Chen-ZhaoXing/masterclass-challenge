🎄 The North Pole is Under Attack! 🎄

Welcome to the Kubernetes Security Masterclass Challenges

📜 Briefing: The Rogue Elf Faction

Tensions are high at the North Pole. A rogue faction of elves is attempting to sabotage the holiday season by hijacking the sleigh's automated routing system and deploying insecure workloads into the North Pole Kubernetes cluster.

As the newly appointed Senior Security Elf, it is your job to stop them. The Chief Holiday Officer is counting on you to lock down the cluster, secure the Master Guidance Coordinates, and ensure that only production-ready, strictly validated workloads are allowed to run.

The holiday season depends on your Kubernetes expertise. Are you ready?

🚀 Available Missions

Warm Up

Trainee Elf

Perfect for your first day. Get comfortable with basic kubectl commands and manifests.

Orientation: YAML Debugging & Image Pinning

OJT: Debugging CrashLoopBackOff & Secrets

Graduation: Services & ConfigMaps

In the Weeds

Workshop Elf

Put your recent training to the test. Apply Dockerfile and Helm best practices.

The Bloated Sleigh Image: Multi-Stage Docker Builds

The Exposed Coordinates: Helm Secret Mounts

The Frozen Handshake: TLS Certificates

Boss Fight

Senior Security Elf

For the daily driver. Handle strict admission controllers and restore wiped stateful apps.

The Trojan Manifest: Policy Engine & RBAC

The Phantom Storage: StatefulSets & PVCs
