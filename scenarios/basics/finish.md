## Trainee Orientation Complete!

Congratulations, Trainee Elf! You've proven you can handle the basics of Kubernetes troubleshooting.

- **Error Messages Are Your Friend:** Every `kubectl` error tells you exactly what's wrong. Read them carefully before searching the internet!
- **Pin Your Images:** Never use `:latest` in production. A pinned tag like `python:3.13-slim` guarantees reproducible, predictable deployments.
- **Namespaces First:** Always ensure the target namespace exists before deploying resources into it. In CI/CD pipelines, namespace creation should be an explicit, automated step.

You are now cleared to attempt the advanced challenges. Good luck, Security Elf!
